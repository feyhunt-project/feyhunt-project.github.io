Data & Privacy Policy
  • What we save: The 3-character triad choices you make and your non-PII country of origin (automatically detected by Cloudflare).
  • What stays private: Your final generated tier list, custom rankings, and progress live entirely in your browser’s local storage. We do not track, save, or upload your personal tier list.
  • What we ignore: We never log your IP address, assign user tracking IDs, or require logins/cookies, ensuring zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is ever collected.
  • Where it goes: Votes are pooled anonymously into a private database used to calculate global character rankings, conduct statistical research, and publish community insights. Each submitted vote contains only the three characters you compared (winner and two others), the time, and your country (no IP address).
